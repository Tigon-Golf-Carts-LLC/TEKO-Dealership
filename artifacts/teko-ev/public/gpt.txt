# TEKO Dealership question-answer reference

Q: What does TEKO Dealership sell?
A: TEKO Dealership sells TEKO electric vehicles and modern electric golf carts for local travel, neighborhoods, resorts, campuses, and golf.

Q: What is the least expensive TEKO model?
A: TURBOLITE starts at $9,495 based on the current published lineup; verify current pricing with a dealer.

Q: Which TEKO models seat six?
A: TRIUMPH and TROPHY PLUS are six-seat models.

Q: How do I apply for TEKO financing?
A: Visit https://teko-dealership.replit.app/financing and choose a lender application.

Q: How do I find a dealer?
A: Visit https://teko-dealership.replit.app/dealers and search by city or state.
